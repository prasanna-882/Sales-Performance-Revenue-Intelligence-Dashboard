
This document explains the **key factors (drivers)** that directly influence sales performance. These drivers are identified using trends and KPIs from the Sales Performance Power BI Dashboard.

---

## 1️⃣ Order Volume

**Description:**

* An increase in the number of orders leads directly to higher revenue.
* Periods with high order counts consistently show strong revenue performance.

**Driver Impact:**

> Revenue growth is primarily driven by **order frequency**, not just pricing.

---

## 2️⃣ Repeat Customers

**Description:**

* Repeat customers place multiple orders over time.
* They contribute more revenue compared to one-time customers.

**Driver Impact:**

> Customer retention is a major long-term revenue driver.

---

## 3️⃣ Product Category Performance

**Description:**

* A small number of product categories generate a large share of total revenue.
* High-performing categories show stable demand and lower return rates.

**Driver Impact:**

> Strong product mix significantly improves overall sales performance.

---

## 4️⃣ Regional Demand

**Description:**

* Certain regions consistently outperform others in revenue and order volume.
* High-performing regions show strong customer engagement.

**Driver Impact:**

> Regional focus and localization strategies can boost sales.

---

## 5️⃣ Discount Strategy

**Description:**

* Moderate discounts increase order volume.
* Excessive discounts do not always increase revenue proportionally.

**Driver Impact:**

> Smart discounting drives sales better than heavy discounting.

---

## 6️⃣ Delivery Efficiency

**Description:**

* Faster deliveries result in higher customer ratings.
* Delayed deliveries increase return rates.

**Driver Impact:**

> Efficient logistics directly influence customer satisfaction and sales.

---

## 7️⃣ Payment Method Convenience

**Description:**

* Certain payment methods are used more frequently than others.
* Easy and trusted payment options increase order completion.

**Driver Impact:**

> Payment convenience reduces cart abandonment and improves sales.

---

## 8️⃣ Customer Experience & Ratings

**Description:**

* Higher customer ratings correlate with repeat purchases.
* Poor ratings are often linked to delivery delays or product issues.

**Driver Impact:**

> Positive customer experience fuels repeat sales and brand loyalty.

---

## ✅ Summary

Sales performance is driven by a combination of **customer behavior, product strategy, regional demand, operational efficiency, and pricing decisions**. Optimizing these drivers can significantly improve revenue and long-term growth.
